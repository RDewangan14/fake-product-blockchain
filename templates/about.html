Project Introduction

Nowadays, Blockchain technology has a great demand in today’s market. It gained a lot of interest over the past few years. Blockchain does not have any trusted intermediary. The attention toward blockchain technology is increasing and it is known for faster transactions and adding more transparency. The contents of its data are tamper-proof.

A customer thinks many times before purchasing a product, having questions whether the product is fake or real. In order to stop the manufacturing and circulation of fake products, it is important to bring transparency about the products to the notice of the consumers. This system delivers verified original products to the customer and empowers consumers to identify fake vs real items via digital QR verification.

Main Objectives & Purpose

1. Anti-Counterfeit Architecture: Design an Anti-Counterfeit system leveraging decentralized Blockchain ledger concepts.
2. QR Code Digital Identity: Secure product details using uniquely encrypted and mined QR codes.
3. Client Transparency: Provide tamper-proof verified data directly to end-consumers before purchase.
4. Immutability & Security: Leverage SHA-256 cryptographic linkage to prevent unauthorized database tampering.
5. Demolishing Counterfeit Networks: Stop the making and sale of duplicate goods by establishing transparent provenance.
6. Consumer Empowerment: Enable instant barcode/QR verification via mobile phones or webcams.

Feasibility Study

Technical Feasibility

Built on Python with standard cryptographic libraries (hashlib SHA-256), Flask framework, and QR encoding algorithms. Runs seamlessly across standard platforms.

Economic Feasibility

Operates on existing computing infrastructure and commodity smartphones without requiring expensive proprietary hardware scanners or servers.

Operational Feasibility

Straightforward web-based UI requiring zero installation for consumers. Intuitive 1-click camera scanning and printable QR code generation.
